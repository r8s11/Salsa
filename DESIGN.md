# Design System: Salsa Segura

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Three worlds, one dance publication"**

Ritmo Vivo serves dancers in the public guide and shop. The Listings Desk is the work surface for operators. The Sleeve is exported artwork, not a site UI theme.

The [full design reference](.impeccable/design-full.md) preserves detailed rules and component guidance. Its historical Stitch palette is reference material; the frontmatter here and the live token sources below describe the implemented palette.

## Colors

- **Ritmo Vivo:** deep navy surfaces, pale text, rose primary actions, and gold secondary accents. See [`src/styles/global.css`](src/styles/global.css) and the [shop palette](src/features/shopify/shop.css).
- **Event types:** reuse the color families in [`calendarsConfig.ts`](src/features/events/model/calendarsConfig.ts). Keep a visible text label alongside color.
- **Listings Desk:** use [`admin.css`](src/styles/admin.css) for operator surfaces and state colors. Reserve state colors for their named states.
- **The Sleeve:** use the [poster palette](src/features/events/components/event-modal/ShareableEventPoster.css) for export artwork only. Keep its dark, cream, rose, and mustard fields out of app controls.

## Typography

Epilogue carries display type and interface labels; Be Vietnam Pro carries body copy. Barlow Condensed and Shrikhand serve the retail or poster roles listed in frontmatter. Great Vibes is reserved for the wordmark.

## Layout

Public discovery keeps city context visible. On narrow screens and coarse pointers, use the calendar's responsive view defaults. Operator screens keep decisions close to the listing.

## Elevation & Depth

Ritmo Vivo may use tonal layers and restrained glass. The Desk uses rules and flat surfaces, not stacked cards. The Sleeve prints flat without shadows.

## Shapes

Use the radius scale in frontmatter. Reserve pill shapes for controls and chips, not page scaffolding.

## Components

- Reuse shared buttons, links, and inputs before styling local variants. Use [`RubberSegment`](src/components/ui/RubberSegment.tsx) for the existing segmented pattern.
- Reuse [Desk primitives](src/components/desk/) for operator workflows.
- Check the [component library](design/components/README.md) for examples. Import the live source, not the reference snapshots.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Keep discovery read-only and submission behind its existing protected flow.
- Follow the dance-night date rule: starts before 05:00 local belong to the previous dance night.
- Do not use color alone to distinguish event types or review states.
- Do not use glyphs or emoji in place of drawn icons.
- Do not nest operator cards or apply public glow and gradients to the Desk.
