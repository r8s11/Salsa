# Claim a listing and manage its team

For school owners, venue owners and managers, artists, DJs, and the people who manage their listings. Last checked locally November 10, 2026.

A listing workspace is separate from your personal profile and from organizer event access. For events, use the [organizer guide](organizers.md).

## Find the right listing

Start with the relevant directory:

| Listing | Directory | Public page address starts with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School | [Schools](https://www.salsasegura.com/schools) | `/s/` |
| Venue | [Venues](https://www.salsasegura.com/venues) | `/v/` |
| Artist or instructor | [Artists](https://www.salsasegura.com/instructors) | `/i/` |
| DJ | [DJs](https://www.salsasegura.com/djs) | `/dj/` |

Open the listing and check its name, city, and details before claiming it. Claiming an existing listing does not create a new one.

- **Missing:** [request the listing](#request-a-missing-listing).
- **Duplicated, or representing the wrong person or business:** [contact Salsa Segura](https://www.salsasegura.com/contact) with the correct details.

## Request a missing listing

You need to be signed in. Schools, venues, artists, and DJs can be requested; organizers use the organizer request in the [organizer guide](organizers.md).

1. Search the directory for the listing first. If it is there, claim it instead.
2. At the bottom of the directory, select **Not listed? Request a listing**, or open [Request a listing](https://www.salsasegura.com/request-listing).
3. Choose the **Type of listing** and enter its **Name**. As you type, **Possible matches** lists directory entries with a similar name. If one is yours, open it and claim it.
4. Add the **City**, **Website** (a full `https://` link), **Instagram**, and **Anything that helps us verify it**. Do not include passwords or private identity documents.
5. Tick **I searched and this listing is not here**, then select **Send request**.
6. Look for **Request sent. We will review it; you can follow it below.** The request appears under **Your listing requests** as **Waiting for review**.

Nothing is published before Salsa Segura reviews the request. A request for a name that already exists in the same city is refused; search for that listing and claim it.

An approved request shows **Listing added** with a **Claim it** link. Approval adds the listing; it does not make you its owner. Follow the link and [submit a claim](#submit-a-claim) to manage it.

## Submit a claim

1. [Sign in](https://www.salsasegura.com/signin), or create your personal account first.
2. Return to the listing's public page. Near the bottom, select **Claim this school**, **Claim this venue**, or **Claim this profile**.
3. Under **Your relationship**, select the option that describes you:
   - Schools and venues: **I own it**, **I manage it**, or **I work there**.
   - Artists and DJs: **This is me** or **I manage it**.
4. In **Message (optional)**, explain your connection and give details the team can use to verify it. The limit is 2,000 characters. Do not include passwords or private identity documents.
5. Select **Submit claim**.
6. Look for **Claim submitted** and **Your claim is waiting for review.**

The claim form states what approval unlocks: profile editing and a view of the upcoming nights linked to the listing, plus timetable, private lessons, and prices for a school. Approval does not give access to organizers' events.

A pending claim does not grant editing access. Revisit the listing while signed in to check its state; do not assume an email will arrive.

### After review

- **Approved:** the listing offers **Open your workspace**. You can also find it under **Your listings** on the [Host desk](https://www.salsasegura.com/host).
- **Rejected:** the listing says **Your previous claim was rejected.** Read any review note. Correct the explanation before submitting another claim, or contact Salsa Segura if you need clarification.
- **Already on the team:** open the workspace rather than sending another claim.

Approval normally grants Owner access if the listing has no active owner, or Manager access if it already has one. Selecting a relationship on the claim form is not a guarantee of a particular permission level.

## Understand listing permissions

These permissions apply to a school, venue, artist, or DJ listing—not to an organizer's events.

| Task | Owner | Manager | Editor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View the listing workspace and its overview | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Change the listing's editable profile fields | Yes | Yes | No |
| Maintain a school's timetable, private lessons, and prices | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add people, change team roles, or remove people | Yes | No | No |
| Ask for a change to the listing name, city, or public page address | Yes, under **Requests** | Yes, under **Requests** | No |
| Ask for an event to be added to an artist or DJ profile | Yes, under **Requests** | Yes, under **Requests** | No |
| Change the listing's publication status | Contact Salsa Segura | Contact Salsa Segura | Contact Salsa Segura |

Only schools have timetable, private-lesson, and price-plan sections. Editor access on a venue, DJ, or artist listing does not add an event editor or a booking tool.

## Add a teammate

Only a listing Owner can do this.

1. Ask the person to create a Salsa Segura account first. Use the email address attached to that account.
2. Open your listing's workspace, then **Team**.
3. Under **Add someone**, enter their **Email**.
4. Choose a **Role**. Use Manager for someone maintaining the public profile, or Editor for someone maintaining a school's offerings. Give Owner access only to someone who should also control team membership.
5. Select **Add to team**.
6. Check that the person appears in the team list. Ask them to sign in and open the listing or their Host desk.

This adds an existing account directly; it is not an invitation for someone who has not registered. Do not share one account among staff.

## Change or remove access

- **Change a role:** in **Team**, choose a different role beside the person's name. Check the result after it saves.
- **Remove access:** select **Remove** beside their name, then confirm **Remove from team**. This removes access to this listing, not their personal Salsa Segura account.
- **Hand over ownership:** add or promote the next Owner before removing or demoting yourself. The listing must retain at least one Owner.

## Ask for a change or an event link

Owners and Managers have a **Requests** section in the listing workspace. Editors do not.

- **Name, city, or page address:** under **Ask for a change to name, city or page address**, fill in **New name**, **New city**, or **What should change and why** (use this for a new page address), then select **Send request**. At least one field is needed. You can also reach this from **Request a change** on the **Profile** page.
- **An event on an artist or DJ profile:** under **Ask for an event to be added to** your profile name, use **Find an event** to search approved events by title, choose the event, add an optional **Note for the reviewer**, and select **Send request**. The event's organizer keeps control of the event; an approved request only adds it to the profile.
- **Venues and schools** cannot request event links. Events at a venue are managed by their organizers. Claiming or managing a venue does not give access to other organizers' events.

### Follow a request

**Requests for** your listing name, and **Your listing requests** on the request page, show each request with its status. Status is shown here only; do not wait for an email.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting for review** | Salsa Segura has not decided yet. Select **Withdraw** if you no longer need it. |
| **Listing added** | The missing listing is in the directory. Use **Claim it** to ask to manage it. |
| **Event linked** | The event now appears on the artist or DJ profile. |
| **Change made** | The name, city, or page address was updated. |
| **Not approved** | The request was declined. Read the **Note** if one is shown. |
| **Withdrawn** | You withdrew it. |

You can have 10 requests waiting for review at a time. Withdraw one or wait for a decision before sending another. A repeat of a request that is still waiting is refused.

## When something is not available

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No claim button while signed out | Use **Sign in** on the listing, then return to it. |
| A claim waiting for review | Wait for the review; revisit the same listing for its status. |
| No Profile or Requests section | Check your role. Editors cannot change the public profile or send requests. |
| No Team section | Only Owners manage listing teams. Ask an Owner to make the change. |
| No listing under Your listings | Check that you used the correct account. If your claim is still pending, access has not been granted. |
| A new teammate cannot be found | They need an existing Salsa Segura account with the email you entered. |
| A listing needs a new name, city, or page address | Use **Requests** in its workspace. Do not claim a different listing as a workaround. |
| A listing needs an image field not offered by its editor, or a status change | Send its public page link and requested change to Salsa Segura. |
