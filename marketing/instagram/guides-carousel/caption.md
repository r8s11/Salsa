## Caption

New to Salsa Segura? You can browse public events and listings without an account.

Swipe to see what you can do:
→ Find dancing by city, date, event type and dance style
→ Add an event to your calendar, copy its link, or send the poster to friends
→ Submit a night. Signed in with a confirmed email, it goes live right away; guest submissions are reviewed first
→ Keep a school, venue or DJ page up to date
→ Claim a listing and give your team their own access

Start with the calendar. Sign up when you want to track your submissions or manage a listing you represent. Questions? Use the contact page, and never send your password or a sign-in code.
