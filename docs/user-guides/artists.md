# Artists and instructors

Maintain your teaching profile and check the classes and events associated with it. Last checked November 6, 2026.

Salsa Segura uses **Artist** for this listing type in the workspace, while the directory and some links use **instructor**. This is separate from your personal account profile.

## Get access to your artist profile

1. Find your listing in the [artist/instructor directory](https://www.salsasegura.com/instructors).
2. Open the profile and check that it represents you.
3. Sign in and select **Claim this profile** near the bottom.
4. Choose **This is me** or **I manage it**, add a useful verification message, and select **Submit claim**.
5. Once approved, select **Open your workspace**, or find the Artist listing under **Your listings** on the [Host desk](https://www.salsasegura.com/host).

[Listing access and teams](listing-access.md) explains pending/rejected claims and permissions. If you do not have a listing, search the directory first, then use **Not listed? Request a listing** at the bottom of it. Enter your professional name, city, and website, and say which schools or events you teach with under **Anything that helps us verify it**. [How listing requests work](listing-access.md#request-a-missing-listing).

## Update your public information

Owner and Manager members can edit the artist profile.

1. Open the artist workspace, then **Profile**.
2. Update any of these fields:

   | Field | What it is for |
   | --- | --- |
   | Bio | Your teaching background and useful information about your work. |
   | Image link | A full `https://` link to a portrait you have the rights to use. This is not a file-upload control. |
   | Website | Your complete professional website address. |
   | Instagram | Your handle or Instagram profile link. |
   | Organization | Your professional affiliation, where applicable. |

3. Select **Save profile** and wait for **Saved.**
4. Return to the public artist page. Check the displayed bio, portrait, and links.

The name, city, public page address, and listing status are controlled by Salsa Segura. To correct the name, city, or page address, select **Request a change** on the Profile page, or open **Requests** and use **Ask for a change to name, city or page address**. Editing your personal display name does not change the artist listing. Not every saved field has a separate label on the current public page.

## Check where you are listed

The public artist profile can show:

- **Teaching next**: upcoming linked classes or events.
- **Past nights**: past linked events.
- **Listed alongside**: related people, organizations, and places when available.

The workspace **Overview** shows **Upcoming nights** linked to the listing. Open a night to check its public details.

These sections reflect existing relationships in Salsa Segura. The Artist workspace does not have a self-service schedule editor, a teaching-style selector, or controls to link and unlink events directly. Owners and Managers can ask for an approved event to be added under **Requests**.

### Correct a class or event

1. Find the event or school's public page.
2. For a school's weekly class, ask the school to correct its timetable entry. A teacher's name can appear as plain text; it is not always a link to an artist profile.
3. For an event you teach at that is missing from your profile, open **Requests** in your artist workspace. Under **Ask for an event to be added to** your name, use **Find an event**, choose the event, add a **Note for the reviewer** if it helps, and select **Send request**. The request shows **Waiting for review**, then **Event linked** once approved. Only Owners and Managers can send it, and the event's organizer keeps control of the event.
4. If the event itself is missing, ask its organizer to list it or use [Submit an event](https://www.salsasegura.com/submit). Only approved events can be found.
5. To remove an incorrect association, or to link a school's class to your profile, contact Salsa Segura with your artist-profile link and the event or school link.
6. Check the affected public pages after the change.

Changing your **Organization** field or mentioning a school in your bio does not grant access to the school's workspace or automatically link its classes to you.

## Let someone help manage your profile

An Owner can use **Team** to add an existing Salsa Segura account as Manager. A Manager can maintain the bio, image link, affiliation, and contact links, but cannot manage the team. Editors cannot change artist profile fields.

Follow [the team instructions](listing-access.md#add-a-teammate). If you also own a school or produce events, those permissions must be granted separately; see [schools](schools.md) and [organizers](organizers.md).

## Bookings and lesson enquiries

Keep your website and Instagram current so students can reach you through the channels shown on your public page. An artist listing does not itself reserve lesson times, take payments, or confirm a student's booking.

## Before sharing your profile

Check your portrait and links on a phone, make sure upcoming events belong to you, and report missing or wrong associations with the relevant page links. If **Profile** is missing from the workspace, confirm your role with the listing's Owner.
