Scope: the shareable event poster (ShareableEventPoster), exported as a 1080×1920 Instagram Story and a 1080×1350 feed cut from the event modal's "Send to friends" action. Visitor mode: Persuade. The person persuaded is the friend who sees it in a story or group chat.

Audience: dancers in Boston and NYC sending a night to their crew. Job: make a friend want to come and get them to the exact event. Proof: the event's real facts (title, date, time, venue, price, styles, host) and its flyer. Constraint: the poster must work without a flyer (on-brand SVG fallback art) and with long titles. It is the one public artifact allowed its own look, separate from Ritmo Vivo.

Seed key: 3d83fbfd (direction scope, persuade, chosen card: pick).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every night is pressed as its own salsa LP. The front of the sleeve sells the night and the back lists its facts like a track list. It refuses the category-default event story: full-bleed flyer, dark scrim, facts in the lower third.

OWN-WORLD: 1970s Fania-era sleeve printing. Flat spot-colour fields (red, mustard, midnight, one per event type), a cream back-cover stock, fat Shrikhand lettering for titles only, Barlow Condensed for the track list with tabular figures and dotted leaders, a record-label masthead with a catalogue line. No glass, no glow, no gradients.

STORY: A friend sees a record cover, not an ad, reads the night's name first, then scans the side-A list for when, where and how much. The QR and short link take them to the exact event.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Story, top to bottom: label masthead band; a square front cover (flyer art in a thick field-colour frame, title lettered in a cover band); the back-cover stock with SIDE A (date, time, venue, price) and SIDE B (QR, short link, host credit). Feed cut: square cover left-aligned, the spine running vertically at the right, the track list below.

FORM: Pick card (Fania Sleeve) over the rolled Clave Count Sheet. The familiarity risk is managed: no vinyl disc anywhere, so it never repeats the homepage record. Kept from the declined hand: one accent reserved for one meaning (the field colour codes event type), and tabular figures pinned to the list grid.

MEMORABLE MOMENT: The track list. "A1 Thursday 24 September … A2 7:30–11:30 PM", with dotted leaders and a catalogue number, is the poster's own vocabulary for event facts.

Unresolved: none. The short link uses /e/<first 8 hex of the event id>, resolved client-side.
