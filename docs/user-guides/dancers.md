# Dancers and general users

Find a night out, discover teachers and schools, share an event, and manage your personal account. Last checked locally November 9, 2026; release requires the migrations listed in [implementation status](improvements.md#verification-and-rollout).

## Find an event

1. Open the [calendar](https://www.salsasegura.com/calendar).
2. Choose your city. Check the city whenever results look unfamiliar or empty.
3. Move to the date or period you want. Switch calendar views when useful; smaller screens start with a list.
4. Search for an event, venue, or keyword. Use the event-type and dance-style filters. On smaller screens, open **Filters** to see them.
5. Open an event to check its date, time, location, price, and host information. Use **View full details** for the event's own page.

The homepage also supports city and neighborhood discovery. In NYC, borough selection sits between the city and neighborhood. Selecting a broader area includes its neighborhoods.

If no results appear, clear your search and filters and check another date. An empty result is not a statement that every venue in the city is closed.

## Tickets, calendars, and sharing

From the event preview:

- **Get Tickets** or **RSVP · Free**, when shown, opens the host's external page. Complete any reservation or purchase there. Opening a Salsa Segura listing is not a reservation or proof of admission.
- **Add to calendar** opens a calendar action. It may open Google Calendar or download a calendar file, depending on the event. Confirm the details before saving; return to the listing for later changes.
- **Copy link** copies the event address for a message or post.
- **Send to friends** prepares the event poster for your device's share options. If sharing is unavailable, the app offers a download instead.
- Open the flyer/poster preview to choose **Story · 9:16** or **Feed · 4:5**, then **Send this poster** or **Save image**.

If an event has no ticket or RSVP link, check its contact details or contact the host. Do not assume it accepts walk-ins or is sold out. Confirm schedule and entry requirements with the host before travelling.

## Discover the people and places behind the events

Use [Discover](https://www.salsasegura.com/discover), or go directly to [Schools](https://www.salsasegura.com/schools), [Artists](https://www.salsasegura.com/instructors), [DJs](https://www.salsasegura.com/djs), or [Venues](https://www.salsasegura.com/venues).

School pages can show **Weekly classes**, **Private lessons**, and **Prices**. A weekly class is a repeating timetable entry, not a confirmed seat or a separate calendar event. Contact the school through its listed channels to confirm changes, availability, and payment arrangements.

## Create an account or sign in

1. Open [Sign in](https://www.salsasegura.com/signin).
2. To use email, enter your email address. The password field appears after you start entering an email.
3. Enter your password, then select **Sign In**. The eye control changes password visibility; it does not reset your password.
4. To create an account, select **Sign up**, enter your email, password, and confirmation, then select **Sign Up**. Passwords must be at least six characters; use a strong, unique password.
5. If the app asks you to confirm your email, check that inbox and follow the confirmation link. Use the resend option when offered.
6. If **Set up your profile** appears, complete the required display name, available username, and city. Usernames use 3–24 letters, numbers, or underscores. Add the optional photo and bio if you want, then finish setup.

Google or Facebook sign-in may also appear when enabled. Select the provider and finish its sign-in process. If it refuses access or is unavailable, use your email sign-in or [contact Salsa Segura](https://www.salsasegura.com/contact).

Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your account email to request a reset link. Check your inbox and spam folder. Never share that link.

## Update your personal profile

1. Open [your profile](https://www.salsasegura.com/profile), then **Edit profile**.
2. Update your display name, bio, city, dance styles, Instagram, and website as needed.
3. Use the photo controls for a profile or cover image. Supported uploads are JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB. Follow any crop or upload prompts and wait for the result.
4. Review **Notifications** and **Privacy** before saving.
5. Select **Save Changes** and check for the saved confirmation.

Your username is read-only after setup in the current editor. Changing your display name does not change your sign-in email or a school, venue, artist, or DJ listing.

### Your public member page

When your profile is public and you have a username, the editor shows your address under **Username**: `salsasegura.com/u/your-username`. Anyone with that address can open the page, including visitors who are not signed in. It shows your display name, photos, bio, city, dance styles, Instagram and website links, and the month you joined. It never shows your email. It is a member profile, not an artist, DJ, school, or venue listing.

### Privacy and preferences

The editor provides these privacy switches:

- **Make my profile public.** Switch it off and your member page shows **Member not found** to everyone else, and the editor stops showing the address.
- **Show my activity numbers on my profile.** When on, your public page shows how many approved events you have hosted. Pending and total submission counts are never public.

Both switches start on. On your own profile, use **Everyone** under **Your numbers** to preview what visitors see.

The only optional notification is **Weekly digest of what's on**, off by default. It is still marked **Not sending yet**: you can save a choice for launch, but no weekly-delivery schedule is promised yet. The city-event and submission-update switches were removed. Submission emails go to the address you entered on that submission; account and security messages go to your account email. Once delivery launches, a digest's unsubscribe link will affect only that optional email.

The personal profile page shows your own account and submission activity. It is not the workspace for your business or artist listing; use [listing access](listing-access.md) for that.

## Suggest an event

1. Open [Submit an event](https://www.salsasegura.com/submit). Submissions must be open for the form to be available.
2. Optionally add a flyer. JPEG, PNG, and WebP images up to 5 MB are supported. You can also type the details without a flyer.
3. If you are signed in, use **Extract Event Details** when offered. Check every extracted date, city, time, price, and name before submitting; automatic extraction can be wrong.
4. Complete the event fields and fix any highlighted errors. Choose the correct event types and dance styles. If you select multiple event types, the first is the primary type.
5. Review any suggested venue, organizer, artist, or school matches. A suggested match is not a claim of ownership.
6. Select **Submit Event**, or **Submit for review** if that is the label shown for your account.
7. Wait for the submission confirmation. Submitting is not the same as publication.

You may be able to submit as a guest when submissions are open. Guests supply contact details, but signing in before submitting makes it possible to track submissions from the same account.

### Track or correct your submission

Open [your profile](https://www.salsasegura.com/profile), then **My submissions**.

| Status | What it means and what you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Waiting for review. Use **Edit** if you need to correct it. |
| Rejected | Not approved. Use **Edit** to revise it and follow the form's instructions. |
| Approved | Published. Use its public-event link to check the result. For changes not offered on your account, contact Salsa Segura. |

Check whether an event already exists before submitting it again. If you submitted while signed out and it does not appear on your profile, contact Salsa Segura with the event details and the email used for the submission.

## Protect your account

Open [Account](https://www.salsasegura.com/account) for sign-in and security settings.

- **Change email:** enter your new email. Confirm using the links sent to both your current and new inboxes before the change takes effect.
- **Sign out on this device:** ends the current session.
- **Sign out other devices:** leaves this session open and ends the others. An already-issued access token may work until it expires.
- **Sign out everywhere:** includes this browser and asks for confirmation.

For permanent deletion, read the [data-deletion page](https://www.salsasegura.com/data-deletion). Eligible accounts can use **Account → Danger zone → Delete account**, type `DELETE` in the confirmation dialog, and confirm. Deletion is irreversible. Accounts tied to protected events, listings, uploads, or operational history may need assistance instead. Removing a social-provider connection does not delete your Salsa Segura account.

## Get help

Use [Contact Salsa Segura](https://www.salsasegura.com/contact) with the page address, error text, and what you expected to happen. For ticketing or entry questions, contact the event host instead. Never send passwords or recovery links.
