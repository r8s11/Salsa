# DJs

Maintain your DJ listing, help dancers find your links, and check the gigs associated with your profile. Last checked November 6, 2026.

Your DJ listing is separate from your personal Salsa Segura account. Editing your personal profile does not update the DJ page.

## Get access

1. Find yourself in the [DJ directory](https://www.salsasegura.com/djs) and open your public profile.
2. Sign in, then use **Claim this profile** near the bottom of the page.
3. Choose **This is me** or **I manage it**, add a useful verification message, and select **Submit claim**.
4. After approval, select **Open your workspace**, or open the listing under **Your listings** on the [Host desk](https://www.salsasegura.com/host).

See [listing access and teams](listing-access.md) for pending or rejected claims and for giving a manager access. If no listing exists, search the directory first, then use **Not listed? Request a listing** at the bottom of it rather than claiming someone else's profile. [How listing requests work](listing-access.md#request-a-missing-listing).

## Update your DJ profile

You need Owner or Manager access to this DJ listing.

1. Open the listing workspace and select **Profile**.
2. Update the fields you need:

   | Field | What to enter |
   | --- | --- |
   | Bio | Your DJ background, musical approach, and useful information for dancers or hosts. |
   | Image link | A full `https://` link to a portrait you have the rights to use. This field takes a link, not a file upload. |
   | Website | Your website or professional contact page, as a complete web address. |
   | Instagram | Your handle or Instagram profile link. |

3. Select **Save profile** and wait for **Saved.**
4. Open your public profile and check the bio, portrait, and external links. Refresh the page if you still see the old information.

If the save button is disabled, check whether you changed a value or whether a save is still running. If a link is rejected, use the full web address rather than a bare domain.

Your name, city, public page address, and listing status are controlled by Salsa Segura. To correct the name, city, or page address, select **Request a change** on the Profile page, or open **Requests** and use **Ask for a change to name, city or page address**.

## Check your gigs and related places

Your public profile can show:

- **Upcoming gigs**, including the next listed gig.
- **Past gigs**.
- **Venues and residencies**, when those relationships are available.
- Dance styles associated with your listing.

The workspace **Overview** also lists **Upcoming nights** linked to the profile. Open a night to inspect its public event details.

These sections depend on event links already recorded in Salsa Segura. They are not a diary you can add to directly from your DJ workspace. The venue/residency section is not a promise of a recurring booking schedule.

### Add or correct a gig association

1. Check that the event itself is listed, using the [calendar](https://www.salsasegura.com/calendar).
2. If it is missing, submit it through [Submit an event](https://www.salsasegura.com/submit), or ask its organizer to list it. Only approved events can be linked.
3. To add a gig, open **Requests** in your DJ workspace. Under **Ask for an event to be added to** your DJ name, use **Find an event**, choose the event, add a **Note for the reviewer** if it helps, and select **Send request**. Only Owners and Managers can send it.
4. Follow the request under **Requests for** your DJ name: **Waiting for review**, then **Event linked** once approved. Select **Withdraw** if you sent it by mistake.
5. To correct or remove a gig that is already linked, send Salsa Segura the public event link and your DJ-profile link.
6. Check the result on the public DJ profile after the change is made.

Salsa Segura reviews every request, and the event's organizer keeps control of the event. Mentioning a night in your bio is not the same as linking the event to your profile.

## Work with a manager

If you are an Owner, use **Team** to add an existing Salsa Segura account. A Manager can update your DJ bio, image link, and contact links. Only an Owner can change the team. [Step-by-step team instructions](listing-access.md#add-a-teammate).

Do not use Editor access when you expect someone to edit the DJ profile: Editors cannot change public profile fields. Claim approval does not grant organizer event access, and a DJ workspace is not a booking or payment system.

## Before sharing your page

- Check that the portrait loads on your phone and is yours to use.
- Open your website and Instagram links.
- Confirm that the upcoming gigs belong to you.
- Send corrections with both the event and DJ-profile links, not just a flyer or a name.
