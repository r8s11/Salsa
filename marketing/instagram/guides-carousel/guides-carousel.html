Your night

Venue running
its own night?

  1. 1

    Everyone signs up. One account each. Don't share one.

  2. 2

    Claim the venue. After review, its workspace: profile, and team for the Owner.

  3. 3

    Submit the night at salsasegura.com/submit. Signed in, it goes live right away.

  4. 4

    Check your venue page. Events linked to it show as Upcoming and Past.

Your venue page doesn't control other promoters' events.

Start

How to start

Sign up. salsasegura.com/signin, then set up your profile.

Submit an event. salsasegura.com/submit. Signed in, it goes live right away; guests are reviewed first.

Ask for help. salsasegura.com/contact with the page link.

Never send your password, a recovery link or a sign‑in code.
